About Us

Building relationships and communities with excellence.

At M/s. Infinity Construction & Developers, we believe construction is more than just creating structures — it's about shaping lifestyles, strengthening communities, and building lasting relationships. Based in Belagavi, we have grown into a trusted name in the construction and development industry through our commitment to quality, safety, and innovation.

Founded and led by Mr. Sudheer R. Kinekar, an experienced architect and consultant, the company stands for integrity, precision, and forward-thinking design. Under his leadership, we have successfully delivered residential, commercial, and mixed-use projects that exemplify both aesthetic appeal and engineering excellence.

Our team of skilled professionals — including Mr. Vijender Patil (Structural Consultant) and Mr. Ajith Pathak (Interior Designer) — brings together decades of combined expertise to ensure every project meets the highest standards of functionality, durability, and design finesse.

At Infinity, we adopt smart construction practices and leverage modern technologies to optimize efficiency, sustainability, and long-term value. From concept to completion, we focus on delivering projects that not only meet expectations but set new benchmarks for quality and reliability.

Vision

To be a leading construction and development company known for excellence, transparency, and innovation — building spaces that inspire and endure.

Mission

To deliver superior construction services through ethical business practices, cutting-edge technology, and a customer-centric approach — ensuring every project reflects our motto: "Build Safe, Build Smart."
