About Us

A single, accountable partner for every stage of your build.

M/s. Infinity Construction & Developers is a Belagavi-based construction and development company delivering structural, architectural, and interior work across residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional projects. We hold every discipline in-house, so clients work with one accountable team from first sketch to final handover — not a chain of subcontractors.

The company is founded and led by Mr. Sudheer R. Kinekar, a practicing architect and consultant, whose approach to integrity, precision, and forward-thinking design shapes how every project is planned and delivered. Under his leadership, the firm has completed private residences, commercial complexes, and mixed-use developments that hold up equally to an architect's eye and an engineer's checklist.

That leadership is backed by specialist consultants — Mr. Vijender Patil on structural engineering and Mr. Ajith Pathak on interior design — whose complementary expertise means every project is reviewed for safety, durability, and finish before it's called complete.

Whatever the scale — a single-family home, a multi-storey commercial building, an industrial facility, or a full interior renovation — the process stays the same: careful planning, transparent execution, and construction practices built for long-term value.
