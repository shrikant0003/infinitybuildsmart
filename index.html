About Us

Building relationships and communities with excellence — across every sector.

At M/s. Infinity Construction & Developers, we believe construction is more than just creating structures — it's about shaping lifestyles, strengthening communities, and building lasting relationships. Based in Belagavi, we've grown into a trusted name for structural, architectural, and interior work across residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional projects.

Founded and led by Mr. Sudheer R. Kinekar, an experienced architect and consultant, the company is built on integrity, precision, and forward-thinking design. Under his leadership, we've delivered projects spanning private homes, commercial complexes, and mixed-use developments — each one balancing aesthetic appeal with engineering excellence.

Our team of specialists — including Mr. Vijender Patil (Structural Consultant) and Mr. Ajith Pathak (Interior Designer) — brings together deep, complementary expertise across structural engineering, architectural planning, and interior design, so every project meets the highest standards of safety, durability, and finish.

Whatever the scale or sector — a single-family home, a multi-storey commercial building, an industrial facility, or a full interior renovation — we bring the same rigorous process: careful planning, transparent execution, and construction practices built for long-term value.
